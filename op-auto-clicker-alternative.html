Macronaut → OP Auto Clicker alternative

An OP Auto Clicker alternative that can also see the screen

OP Auto Clicker got one thing exactly right: a single small window with an interval, a button, a repeat count and a Start key, and nothing else in your way. Macronaut opens on that same layout on purpose. The difference is what happens when clicking on a timer stops being enough.

The short version. If you click the same spot at a fixed speed and that works, OP Auto Clicker is smaller and does the job. If you keep wishing it would wait until something appears, or click a button that is not always in the same place, that is what Macronaut adds.

The window you already know

Macronaut opens on Basic, a plain auto clicker:

  • Click interval in hours, minutes, seconds and milliseconds, or Max speed to click as fast as the machine can.
  • Button: left or right.
  • Where: at the cursor, or pick a spot on screen and it clicks there every time.
  • Repeat: until stopped, or a set number of times.
  • Start and Stop, with F8 as the hotkey and Esc to stop anything at any time.

No nodes, no wires, nothing to set up. If that is all you want, that is all you ever have to see.

Where a timer runs out

A plain auto clicker clicks a position at a speed. That covers a lot, and it falls short in a few predictable places:

  • The button moves. A window opens somewhere else, a list scrolls, and the clicker keeps hitting the spot where the button used to be.
  • You need it to wait. Click, then wait until the next screen has loaded, then click again. A timer has to guess how long that takes, and guesses either waste time or click too early.
  • You need it to stop on its own. Stop when a word appears, or when a pixel changes colour, rather than after a number you picked in advance.

Macronaut’s Advanced view is a canvas where those become steps: wait for an image and click it where it is, wait for text using Windows’ own OCR, wait for a pixel, loop, and branch on what it found.

What OP Auto Clicker does better

  • Size. It is tiny and opens instantly. Macronaut is 78 MB because it carries a full interface toolkit and image matching, and that is a real cost.
  • Everything in one window. The middle button and double clicks are right there in its main window. In Macronaut they are one step further away, in the Auto-Click step on the canvas.

What Macronaut does better

  • It can look before it clicks. Find a button by what it looks like, wherever it is on screen, and click it there.
  • It does more than click. Press keys, hold a key down while clicking, type a block of text, scroll, drag.
  • It records. Do the task once and get it back as a list of steps you can open and change.
  • You can read the source. An auto clicker installs a global keyboard hook. Macronaut’s code is public, so you do not have to take that on trust.
