A calmer Advanced window, and wires that take the short way round.

**The Advanced window reads as one piece now.** The layout is the same, but
the header, the canvas, the timeline and the bottom bar no longer meet in hard
edges and sudden jumps of colour. The canvas sits as one sunken surface with
the timeline and the run log inside it, instead of parts bolted onto its
sides, and the canvas and timeline follow the theme, so Daylight no longer has
a dark canvas in the middle of a light window.

**Wires take the shortest clear path.** The router used to take the first
free lane it found rather than the nearest, which drew long detours around
nothing. Two steps placed close together, one just right of the other, got a
wire that looped backwards before reaching it; that is now a single S-bend
through the gap.

**Add-step windows keep their size.** Switching between Click, Move, Drag and
Scroll (or between the kinds of Type and Detect) made the window jump in
width. Each family now keeps one width, set by its widest option.

**Clicking elsewhere lets go of a field.** A number or text field in the main
window kept the keyboard after you clicked away from it, so the custom speed
box went on catching keys. Clicking anywhere else now takes the focus off it
and keeps what you typed.
